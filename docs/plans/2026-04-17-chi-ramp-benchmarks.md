## Notes

- Environments are re-initialized (identity init) when chi changes between ramp stages; zero-padding traps CTM in a suboptimal fixed point.
- Chi-ramp only affects the forward CTM pass. For explicit AD, the speedup comes from line-search forward calls. For implicit AD, it speeds up both forward and backward.
- Speedup tapers off at large chi on CPU because the backward (Arnoldi/Neumann) cost dominates. On GPU, the speedup is more consistent (1.2-2.1x).
- Few-step runs (2 steps) with large chi jumps may show small energy differences (< 1e-3) due to different convergence trajectories.
